# Multi-JurisdictionComplianceChallenge

## ShopGhana – Multi-Jurisdiction Compliance Response Matrix

| Element                          | Ghana DPA                                                                     | GDPR (EU)                                                                                 | CCPA/CPRA (California)                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Right to Deletion Exists?**    | ✅ Yes – Abena can request deletion of her personal data.                      | ✅ Yes – Lukas can request erasure under Article 17.                                       | ✅ Yes – Maria can request deletion and to stop the sale of personal info.                                                  |
| **Exemptions / Conditions**      | Data necessary for **legal, accounting, or tax obligations** may be retained. | Exemptions: compliance with **legal obligations**, public interest, or ongoing contracts. | Deletion can be delayed if **data is required to complete ongoing transactions or disputes** (Maria has an active return). |
| **Response Deadline**            | Typically **30 days** under Ghana DPA.                                        | **One month** (can extend by 2 months for complex requests, with notice).                 | **45 days** (can extend 45 more days with notice) under CCPA/CPRA.                                                         |
| **Penalties for Non-Compliance** | Fines & sanctions enforced by the **Data Protection Commission**.             | Fines up to **€20M or 4% of global annual turnover**.                                     | Civil penalties enforced by the **California Privacy Protection Agency**, up to **$7,500 per violation**.                  |
| **Consent Requirements**         | Opt-in consent required for processing personal data.                         | GDPR requires **explicit consent** unless another legal basis applies.                    | Users can opt-out of sale; consent implied for collection unless otherwise restricted.                                     |

## Customer-Specific Analysis & Action Steps

### Customer A: Abena (Accra, Ghana)

* **Legal Right:** ✅ Yes, under Ghana DPA.
* **Obligations:** Delete all personal data unless retention required for **tax, accounting, or legal purposes**.
* **Data Retention:** Can retain transaction logs, invoices, or records required by law; anonymize where possible.
* **Response Deadline:** 30 days.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Identify all Abena's personal data in databases.
  2. Delete PII (names, emails, addresses, payment info) unless legally required to retain.
  3. Confirm deletion via email to Abena.
  4. Document the process internally.
* **Draft Response:**

> "Dear Abena, we have successfully deleted your personal information from our systems, except for data legally required to retain for accounting and tax purposes. Thank you for being a valued customer."

---

### Customer B: Lukas (Berlin, Germany)

* **Legal Right:** ✅ Yes, under GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure).
* **Exemptions:** Can retain data if necessary for **contract fulfillment, legal obligations, or public interest**.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Identify Lukas's personal data across all systems.
  2. Delete or anonymize all data not required for legal obligations.
  3. Confirm deletion within **1 month**; if extension needed, notify Lukas with reasons.
  4. Document processing and legal justification for any retained data.
* **Response Deadline:** 1 month (extendable to 3 months with notice).
* **Consequence of Missing Deadline:** GDPR fines, potential reputational damage, enforcement by supervisory authority.
* **Draft Response:**

> "Dear Lukas, your request to erase your personal data has been processed. Certain information may be retained for legal obligations (e.g., accounting), which cannot be deleted. All other personal data has been removed from our systems."

---

### Customer C: Maria (Los Angeles, California)

* **Legal Right:** ✅ Yes, under CCPA/CPRA.
* **Exemptions:** Deletion may be **delayed due to active return dispute**.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Identify all Maria's personal data.
  2. Process "stop selling" request immediately: remove her data from any third-party marketing/sales channels.
  3. Delay deletion of data directly tied to the dispute until resolution.
  4. Notify Maria about what can be deleted immediately and what must be temporarily retained.
  5. Document all actions and communications.
* **Response Deadline:** 45 days (extendable by 45 more days with notice).
* **Draft Response:**

> "Dear Maria, we have processed your request to stop selling your personal information to third parties. Your deletion request is temporarily delayed due to an ongoing return dispute; once resolved, your personal information will be deleted in accordance with CCPA/CPRA requirements."

---

## ✅ Key Implementation Notes

* Maintain **audit logs** of all deletion requests.
* Communicate clearly with customers about exceptions and timelines.
* Ensure internal staff are trained on **multi-jurisdictional compliance**.
* Apply **least privilege access** during deletion to prevent accidental exposure.
